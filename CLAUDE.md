# CLAUDE.md

Architecture, layout, database formats, hashtables, local regeneration and the CI
automation are documented in `README.md` - read it when you need them. Format specs
live in `docs/meta-db-format.md` and `docs/database.md`.

- Everything under `db/` is generated by `scripts/db_build.py`. Never hand-edit it.
- A change to `dumps/` or `hashes/` must be committed together with the regenerated
  `db/` output, or the Verify Generated DB check fails.
